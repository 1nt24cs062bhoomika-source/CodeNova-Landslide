# CodeNova – Landslide Risk Monitoring System

## 🌍 AI-Based Early Warning and Landslide Risk Monitoring System

CodeNova is an AI-based landslide risk monitoring and early warning system designed to identify potential landslide-prone areas and provide timely alerts.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Landslides can cause loss of life, damage infrastructure, and affect communities in vulnerable regions. Traditional monitoring methods may not always provide timely warnings.

CodeNova aims to provide a centralized platform for monitoring, analyzing, and communicating landslide risks.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

CodeNova provides:

- Landslide risk monitoring
- Risk analysis
- Risk visualization
- Early warning alerts
- Alert management
- Field team information
- Analytics for decision making

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Dashboard
Provides an overview of the current landslide risk situation.

### 🗺️ Risk Map
Helps visualize landslide-prone and high-risk areas.

### 📈 Risk Analysis
Provides analysis of factors related to landslide risk.

### 🚨 Alert Center
Displays active alerts based on risk levels and provides alert details, risk scores, environmental indicators, recommended responses, and authorities notified.

### 👷 Field Teams
Provides information about field teams involved in monitoring and response.

### 📑 Analytics
Provides analytical information to support monitoring and decision making.
